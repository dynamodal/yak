## 2026-10-29: Back up now always says what happened

- The result of **Back up now** (written, failed with the reason, nothing changed) shows right under the buttons, not further down the page.
- With no folder chosen, or a folder that needs permission again, **Back up now** asks for one and then backs up, and a line beside it says so.
- When encrypted backups are on but this device lacks the passphrase, the button is off and a line says to enter the passphrase; nothing unencrypted is written.
- Choosing a folder reports the backup it wrote or the error.
- Forgetting the passphrase on this device updates Backup & restore at once.
