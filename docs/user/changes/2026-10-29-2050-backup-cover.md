## 2026-10-29: A backup by another device counts

- When your devices sync, a recent backup made by another device covers this one if it holds everything this device holds. A phone or Firefox no longer warns "no backup" while your laptop backs up every hour.
- **Backup** in Settings then says "Backed up by <device>, <time>".
- Change something that no recent backup holds and the warning comes back once the last backup is older than your limit, naming the device to open.
- Older versions ignore the new data; nothing needs to be reset.
