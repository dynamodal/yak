## 2026-10-30: Choose how many decimals hours show

- Settings › General › Hours shown as now also offers decimals with 1 place or whole hours, next to 2 places and hours and minutes.
- It changes only what is shown, in the list, week, reports and Home. Stored times are untouched, so rounded totals may not add up exactly.
- Nothing stored changed for existing data; two places stays the default.
