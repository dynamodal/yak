## 2026-10-30: Backups, Version history and Recently deleted, explained

- The backup guide now describes the three safety nets side by side: what each covers, where it is stored, and what restoring from it does.
- "Go back in time" is now called **Version history**. Its points show **Recorded on: This device**.
- Restoring from Version history asks first and says how to undo it. Each restore leaves a **Before restore** point that is never thinned away.
- Replace now names the safety copy and the way back (**Merge it back**), and on a paired device says that only this device changes.
- **Merge it back** stays until you dismiss it or 45 days pass. Nothing stored changed.
