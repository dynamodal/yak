## 2026-10-30: Backups, Version history and Recently deleted are sections of Settings

- **Backups** no longer has a page of its own. Closed, it shows the last backup (or which device made it), any warning and **Back up now**; open it for the folder, encrypted backups and restore.
- **Version history** (go back in time, and the warning when many entries are deleted or changed) is its own section; the warning settings moved here from Display.
- **Recently deleted** says what can be brought back, where, and for how long.
- The **Passphrase** line stays its own line, now in **Passphrase and import**, with **Manage** for what it protects and forgetting it on this device.
- Old links to Backup & restore still work and open the right section.
