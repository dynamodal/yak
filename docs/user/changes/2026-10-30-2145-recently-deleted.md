## 2026-10-30: Recently deleted lists everything

- **Settings › Recently deleted** lists every kind of deleted item: time entries, notes and tasks, projects, clients, tags and files in notes, each with **Restore**.
- Deleted files keep their contents for 45 days, so a restored file opens again; before, a deleted file's contents went at once.
- **Delete for good** removes an item on all your devices; it asks first.
- New setting **Delete files for good straight away**, for all your devices, if you prefer the old behaviour for files.
- Projects, clients, tags and files you deleted earlier show "date unknown" and stay until you delete them for good. Nothing to do on your devices; older versions of Yak keep working.
