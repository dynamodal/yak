## 2026-10-31: Devices find a direct connection more often, and say why when they cannot

- Devices on different networks can now connect directly when the routers allow it, instead of always syncing through the relay.
- A device that syncs through the relay says why on its row in Settings, for example that both browsers hide their local addresses.
- **Connection report** (a device page, under Advanced) lists the recent tries without names or addresses, ready to paste into a problem report.
- While on the relay, devices look for a direct path again after 1, 2, then every 5 minutes, and at once when the network changes.
