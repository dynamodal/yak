## 2026-11-04: Drag entry edges on the Calendar

- Drag the top or bottom edge of a block to change when an entry starts or stops; it snaps to 5 minutes and shows the new time while you drag.
- Undo is offered after each drag; Esc cancels it. A running entry has no stop handle.
- On a phone, tap a block once to show its handles; tap it again to open the editor.
