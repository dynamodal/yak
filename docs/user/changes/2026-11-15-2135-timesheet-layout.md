## 2026-11-15: Timesheet layout in Reports

- **Reports** has a **Table | Timesheet** switch. The Timesheet is an invoice-style list: a block per project or client with a row per day (weekday, date, note, hours in tenths).
- Each block shows its total, and the period has one total at the end.
- Click hours or a note to copy it; **Copy** copies a line and **Copy all** the block's lines.
- Each day is rounded to the nearest 0.1 h and totals add the rounded days.
