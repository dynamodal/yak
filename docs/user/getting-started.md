# Getting started

Yak is a time tracker with Markdown notes and tasks.
It runs in your browser and keeps your data on this device.
Nothing needs an account or a server.

**Is my data safe?**
- Your data lives only in this browser, so set up a backup folder early (below).
- If you never pair a device or share a space, nothing leaves your computer.
- Clearing site data, a browser profile reset or a failing disk can erase it. A backup is what saves you.

## First start

The welcome says what Yak is and offers two ways to start: **Time tracker** (Notes and Tasks hidden) or **Time, notes and tasks**. Either makes a new account with one space (a separate bucket for entries, notes and tasks), named Personal by default.
The choice only decides what this device shows. Change it any time under **Settings › Features**; hidden data is kept.
The **Help** link in the header opens Help and About before you start.

Already use Yak? The small line below opens two more ways:

- **Join my other device** takes the account of a device you already use. On that device open **Settings**, choose **+ Add a device**, then **Make a one-time code**, and enter or scan it here. That device approves this one.
- **Restore from a backup** opens **Backup & restore** to bring data back from a folder or file.

## Then

1. Unless you chose **Time tracker**, Yak opens on **Home**: what needs you, this week's hours, tasks due soon and recent notes. See [Home](#/help/home).
2. Type what you work on in the **Time** tab and press **Start**.
3. Add a note or a task in **Notes** or **Tasks**.
4. Set up a backup folder (**Settings › Backup and safety**, then **Backup & restore**). Do this early: a browser can lose its data.
5. Optional: add a second device with **+ Add a device** on **Settings**.

## Get it on your phone

1. Open the Yak address in your phone's browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
2. Add it to the Home Screen: **Share › Add to Home Screen** on iPhone, the browser menu › **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** on Android.
3. Open it from the new icon and choose **Join my other device** (see [Devices and sync](#/help/devices)), or **Restore from a backup**.

The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from the browser tab: set it up there, not in the tab.
More in [Browsers and phones](#/help/browsers).
