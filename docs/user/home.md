# Home

**Home** is a summary of where things stand. It is the first tab, and Yak opens on it.

- **Needs you**: what waits for an answer, such as a device asking to join or no backup yet. The same rows as in Settings.
- **This week**: total hours and a bar per day.
- **Due soon**: tasks with a due date, late ones marked. Tick one off right there.
- **Recent notes**: the latest notes with their folder and a line of text.
- **Where the week went**: hours by project.

A card hides when its feature is off or it has nothing to show.
Cards sit three across, two or one, depending on the width.
Each chart has the same numbers as a table.

Home is a switch under **Settings › Features**.
Choosing **Time tracker** when you start leaves it off, and Yak then opens on Time.
