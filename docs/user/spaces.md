# Spaces

A space is a separate bucket of entries, projects, notes and tasks, for example Personal and Work.

- Switch the space from the header. Some pages can show all spaces together.
- **Settings › Spaces** has one row per space. Open a row to see the devices that keep it as toggle chips: tick a chip to make that device keep that space (the device's own row in **Devices and sync** shows the same chips). **+ New space** adds one; open a row to rename, archive or remove it.
- Move a note to another space from its menu; links to it are updated.
- Each device chooses which spaces it keeps. A work laptop can hold Work only, while your phone holds both.

Why spaces exist: a device syncs a whole space or none of it, so what must not reach a device lives in its own space.

## Sharing a space

- Open the space's page (choose its name on **Settings**), then **+ Share <space> with an account** under **Shared with**, then **Make a link**. **+ Share with an account** under **Other accounts** on Settings does the same after you pick a space. If you have no passphrase yet, a sheet asks you to create one (**Create and make a link**); one device is enough. The accounts it is shared with show on the space's row on Settings.
- You make a link that works once, for 15 minutes. They open it in Yak, you both compare a code over a call, and you approve (**Approve** asks for your passphrase if it is locked).
- Opening a share link on a device you have not set up shows the same sheet first; the link is kept for its 15 minutes meanwhile.
- **Other accounts** on Settings lists every account you share with or that shares with you: its name, a short fingerprint and what is shared. Choose one for its page: the full fingerprint, your own name for it (**Rename**; your devices see it, the other account does not), the spaces you share with it (**End…**), the spaces it shares with you (**Leave…**) and **+ Share another space**. That starts the usual link and code comparison for the space you pick; if a different account asks than the one whose page you came from, it says so before you approve.
- A space's page lists **Kept on** (each of your devices, a box to keep the space there, and when it was last seen) and **Shared with** (each account with its fingerprint, since when, and **End…** or **Leave…**; an open link with the time left and **Cancel link**).
- Other accounts can edit and delete in what you share, and data cannot be taken back.
- Only that space's shared part is shared: notes, tasks, projects and files. Time entries, rates and logs stay with each account. Deleting something in a shared space deletes it for everyone in the share.
- Only the account that made the share adds or ends accounts. A member chooses **Leave…** on the space's page or the account's page; a shared space has no **Remove** for them. Whoever is out keeps what they have; the space stays theirs and nothing more is exchanged.
- To share a single note, move it into its own space and share that space. Moving a note copies the files it uses; the first space keeps its own copies.
- Files in a shared space reach each member's devices directly from the other devices, never through the relay.
